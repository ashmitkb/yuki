Our Philosophy

Where snow meets
southern fire.

YUKI — meaning "snow" in Japanese — is a meditation on contrast. We bring the precision and reverence of Japanese omakase culture to Bangalore's vibrant soul, crafting each plate as a moment suspended in time.

Every ingredient is sourced with intention. Every technique is practiced into instinct. Every evening is a conversation between chef and guest, unrepeatable and unforgettable.
